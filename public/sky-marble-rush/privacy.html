Privacy Policy

Sky Marble Rush (the "Game") is developed by Ordino. This policy explains what information the Game handles.

Data collected: There are no accounts, sign-in or personal information forms, and the Game itself sends no data to our servers. The Game is free and shows Google AdMob ads (optional rewarded ads at the end of a level and occasional full-screen ads between levels). To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect from your device: advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA, if you allow it), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interaction data, and device and diagnostic information. This data is processed by Google: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent and your choices: In regions such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the Game shows Google's consent form on first launch; you can decline personalised ads and change your choice later in Settings › “Ad privacy options”. On iPhone you are asked for tracking permission; if you decline, the IDFA is not used and you can still play for free. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Data stored on your device: Game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, selected marble) and settings (sound, language, difficulty, graphics) are stored only locally on your device and are never sent to us. You can delete them by uninstalling the app or with Settings › Reset progress.

Children: The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes: If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
